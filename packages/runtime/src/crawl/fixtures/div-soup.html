Returns policy
You can return any unworn item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with all tags attached.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five business days after the return arrives at our warehouse in Leeds. Return shipping is free for orders over 50 GBP; otherwise a flat fee of 4.95 GBP is deducted from the refund.
Sale items marked as final sale cannot be returned. Gift cards are non-refundable.
